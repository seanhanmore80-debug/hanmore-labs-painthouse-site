HANMORE LABS HELP DESK

Here to help.

PaintHouse VR support and simple ways to get back to painting.

01 / GET STARTED

Having trouble launching?

Make sure the headset has enough free storage, restart your Quest, and open PaintHouse VR again. If needed, update or reinstall the game from the headset's App Library. Please preserve any artwork you wish to keep before clearing app data.

02 / CONTROLS

Tools not responding?

PaintHouse VR is designed around Meta Quest Touch controllers. Check battery levels and controller tracking, then release and re-grab the tool. If a station still behaves unexpectedly, note the name of the station and the action you were trying to perform.

03 / DELETION

Remove saved data

On your Quest, go to Settings and open the Apps or Storage section. Find PaintHouse VR, then clear its stored data or uninstall the app. Menu names vary across software versions. If you use Meta Cloud Backup, manage backup copies using Meta's backup controls. See our Privacy Policy for details.

04 / RECORDING

Capture your adventures

Use Meta Quest's built-in casting, screenshot and recording features. These are controlled by your headset and Meta account, not by a Hanmore Labs upload service.

REPORT A PROBLEM

Tell us what happened.

You can report reproducible game issues on our public GitHub support tracker. Posting an issue requires a GitHub account and the report is public. Never include personal data, passwords, device IDs or private recordings. For data deletion, the self-service steps above do not require GitHub or any account beyond your Quest.
