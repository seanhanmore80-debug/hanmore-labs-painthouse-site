Privacy Policy.

How PaintHouse VR handles gameplay information on Meta Quest.

Publisher: Hanmore Labs • Effective: November 19, 2026
In brief: PaintHouse VR is a single-player creative game designed to work offline after installation. It stores relevant game settings and creations locally on your headset. The game does not transmit paintings or gameplay state to other players or a Hanmore Labs server. Hanmore Labs does not run a separate player-account or gameplay-analytics server.

1. What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to the PaintHouse VR game published by Hanmore Labs on Meta Horizon OS / Meta Quest and to the informational website you are reading. Meta's headset operating system and Store are separate services governed by Meta's own privacy terms.

2. Information processed by the game

For gameplay, PaintHouse VR processes device/controller input and movement information to represent your actions in VR. It processes your in-game paint choices, strokes, created objects, equipment interactions, music mode and game state.

To remember selections and creations, the app may save preferences and gameplay content locally using its app storage, including settings, paint artwork, object customization and session or inventory state. The exact data kept can vary with the game version, features you use and your own save/reset choices.

The initial PaintHouse VR release is single-player and offline. It does not offer multiplayer sessions, local-network discovery or game-created online accounts. PaintHouse VR processes controller input and artwork locally to run its creative activities and save progress.

3. How information is used and shared

Information is processed on your headset to run the game, remember local settings and saves, and respond to controller input. PaintHouse VR does not share your drawings or ordinary gameplay state with other players through an in-game networking service.

Hanmore Labs does not sell player data, run a separate player registration service or operate a third-party advertising network in PaintHouse VR. We do not collect payment card information. Meta processes purchases, downloads, account information, optional operating-system recordings and platform diagnostics under its own policies. Learn more in Meta's Privacy Policy.

If you use your headset's casting, recording or livestreaming features, you control what is captured or shared through those device and third-party services; those recordings are not automatically uploaded to Hanmore Labs.

4. Storage, retention and protection

Gameplay saves and settings are stored in the app's local data area until changed or deleted. Hanmore Labs does not retain a separate central copy of ordinary game saves. Screenshots, recordings or casting you choose to make through the headset are handled by the chosen device or platform services.

No internet service is required for core single-player gameplay after installation, although the Meta Quest operating system and Store may require connectivity for their own services, downloads or updates.

5. Your choices and how to delete data

You can delete your PaintHouse VR app data yourself. On Meta Quest, open the device's app/storage settings, locate PaintHouse VR and choose the option to clear its data or uninstall the app. The exact menu labels vary by headset software version. Uninstalling removes local app-managed game data; copies held by Meta Cloud Backup, if applicable and enabled, are controlled separately through Meta's backup settings.

Because we do not operate player accounts or a game-save server, Hanmore Labs normally has no developer-hosted personal gameplay record to delete. For questions about deleting information or requesting assistance, see our Support and Data Deletion page. Please do not publish private identifiers or sensitive information in a public support report.

6. This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not intentionally add advertising trackers, analytics scripts, login cookies or contact-data forms to these pages. GitHub may process ordinary website access information as the hosting provider under GitHub's Privacy Statement.

7. Children and family use

PaintHouse VR is designed to be playful and family-friendly, but headset use and account eligibility remain subject to Meta's age requirements and parental controls. The game does not ask children to register a separate Hanmore Labs account. We do not knowingly operate a service that collects children's contact details.

8. Changes and questions

We may update this policy if the game gains new services, data flows or features. The current effective date will appear at the top of this page. For questions about our data practices or help with local deletion, start with the Support page.
